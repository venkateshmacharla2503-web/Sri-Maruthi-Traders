# Sri Maruthi Traders

A modern, responsive business website for **Sri Maruthi Traders**, an electrical and building materials store.

The website is designed to provide customers with an easy way to explore electrical products, building materials, and contact the business for product enquiries.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

**Sri Maruthi Traders** provides a range of electrical and building-related products, including:

- LED Lights
- LED Bulbs
- Ceiling Fans
- Exhaust Fans
- Switches & Sockets
- Wires & Cables
- MCBs & Distribution Boards
- Electrical Accessories
- Cement
- Building Materials

This project focuses on creating a professional and user-friendly online presence for the business.
